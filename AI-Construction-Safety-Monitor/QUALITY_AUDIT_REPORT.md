# AI Construction Safety Monitor: technical quality audit

**Decision: not yet verified reliable for worker-level PPE decisions or alerts.** The saved detector evaluation and one video-processing run support a controlled student prototype, not an accuracy or safety claim. The largest gap is independent, representative worker/PPE ownership and event-level ground truth. **Retraining is currently unjustified**: detector errors, incomplete labels and association errors have not been separated sufficiently to choose a targeted training intervention.

Scope: static inspection of source, README, checkpoints, configurations and saved reports; read-only rechecks of class mapping, training-label statistics, balanced-label structure, checkpoint SHA-256 and saved video decoding. No inference, training, test-set evaluation, source/dataset/weight modification or new experimental outputs were performed. Existing output counts are historical snapshots, not a fresh hash-based integrity scan of every source file.

## C. Person detection and association

[`person_detector.py`](src/person_detection/person_detector.py) loads a local checkpoint only, verifies a `person` class and returns boxes/confidence. The local `yolov8n.pt` was checked: 80 classes with person ID 0. [`association.py`](src/monitoring/association.py) uses top 40% of person box for helmet and 20–80% for vest; associates if at least 60% of PPE box overlaps the region; contenders within 0.15 overlap are ambiguous; confidence >=0.5 is required for a decision. One PPE detection cannot be assigned to multiple people. `MISSING` requires NoHelmet/NoVest; no evidence, low confidence, ambiguity or conflict yields `UNKNOWN`. More than one person can geometrically overlap a PPE box, and a score difference beyond the fixed margin can still choose the wrong worker; box containment is not proof of wear. Evidence gaps or low-confidence detections may also suppress otherwise confident results.

[`test_association.py`](tests/test_association.py) contains eight synthetic tests; saved Step 4 results report they passed. [`VALIDATION_REPORT.md`](runs/PPE/step4_person_association/validation/review_20261118_01/VALIDATION_REPORT.md) describes four images with nine assistant-provisional, non-blind, non-exhaustive boxes: 9/9 selected matched persons, 12/18 UNKNOWN item decisions, five matches and five UNKNOWN out of ten provisionally known statuses. One MISSING was against UNKNOWN visual status. These figures are not ground truth, dataset-level recall, or assignment accuracy; there are no independent PPE-ownership labels and no independently labelled known-MISSING cases.

Minimum practical starting set: independently label the existing five priority cases in [`STEP_1_4_DECISION.md`](STEP_1_4_DECISION.md)—single worker, two overlapping workers, occluded group and crowd—with person boxes, visible per-item status and explicit ownership of each relevant PPE box; include explicit NoHelmet and NoVest examples if visible, otherwise add the fewest suitable labelled frames. Score person coverage, correct/wrong ownership, unassigned/UNKNOWN and false MISSING separately. Five cases are a diagnostic start, not a statistical reliability claim; expand to the acceptance sample below.

## D. Video processing

[`process_video.py`](src/detection/process_video.py) uses existing detectors and writes separate JSON and sampled `annotated.mp4`; [`video.py`](src/monitoring/video.py) samples using index/source FPS and catches inference exceptions, emitting `people: null`, not a PPE decision. Invalid frames and read errors are recorded. Source-frame timestamps are estimates for variable-frame-rate sources, and the fixed-output-FPS sampled video cannot preserve all irregular source timing. `VideoWriter.write` is not independently checked per frame, and frame-count metadata may be inaccurate; an exception while writing can lose accumulated per-frame data because the report is updated after `process_capture` returns. Large inputs accumulate all frame results in memory. These are code-review risks, not observed failures in the sample.

[`sample_01/results.json`](runs/PPE/step5_video/sample_01/results.json) reports 206 decoded source frames at 25 FPS, 17 sampled frames at requested 2 FPS, zero errors and 11.59 seconds processing; saved indices/timestamps begin 0/0.00, 13/0.52, 25/1.00, 38/1.52. The saved [`annotated.mp4`](runs/PPE/step5_video/sample_01/annotated.mp4) was opened read-only and all 17 frames decoded at 2 FPS and 3840×2160. Successful decoding is not accuracy verification or a real-time claim. Tests in [`test_video.py`](tests/test_video.py) use synthetic frames/failures; codec corruption, inaccurate FPS/frame count, VFR, longer videos and real inference failures remain unverified.

The user-reported visual review of the first frame in [`visual_review.md`](runs/PPE/step5_video/safety_rules_demo_01/visual_review.md) confirms, for that frame only, that the right worker lacks a vest and wears a helmet, while the left worker wears both. The screenshot was not provided with the documentation update; this is a reported human observation, not independent review of all 17 frames, event duration or model accuracy. Further labelled clips must cover compliant and missing PPE, uncertain/occluded views, nearby people and transitions.

## E. Safety rules

[`ppe_rules.py`](src/safety_rules/ppe_rules.py) requires `MISSING`, no per-item explanation, and matching associated NoHelmet/NoVest evidence with confidence >=0.5; confident contradictory evidence suppresses an incident. UNKNOWN/PRESENT and failed frames do not create incidents. Tests in [`test_ppe_rules.py`](tests/test_ppe_rules.py) cover these branches and deduplication; the prior test run reported 19 total tests passed, which verifies selected logic, not real-world correctness.

Deduplication uses a unique mutual person-box IoU >=0.5 across adjacent sampled frames and contiguous per-item missing evidence. Per-frame detector IDs are not identities. Absence, ambiguity, UNKNOWN, failures or timing gaps reset continuity and may create duplicates when evidence reappears; similar boxes can also falsely link different workers and suppress a real incident. A single confident model prediction triggers a candidate without temporal confirmation, so a wrong detector class or wrong owner can create a false incident. The separate [`incidents.json`](runs/PPE/step5_video/safety_rules_demo_01/incidents.json) holds one first-frame NoVest candidate and labels it unverified; user review supports that particular frame only. No event-level false incident, missed event, duplicate or identity-swap rates are established. Rules can return `complete` when a malformed saved report has no frames but advertises complete status; input validation and failure propagation need explicit tests before any alert use.

## Minimal remediation and measurable prototype acceptance

These are **proposed engineering gates**, not existing approved project requirements or a guarantee of safety. Agree target values with the project team before using results to make a release claim.

1. **Human review first:** confirm label policy and adjudicate the five existing priority cases plus enough additional cases to include both NoHelmet and NoVest; document one owner per PPE box and mark unobservable as UNKNOWN. Investigate 00269 and sparse-label crowds in a separate review record, without silently editing the benchmark.
2. **Small independent evaluation:** freeze a review set of at least 20 distinct scene/time segments and at least 50 assessable worker instances, including at least 10 crowded/occluded instances and at least 10 independently visible missing-PPE instances covering both types. Report denominators, person recall at IoU 0.5, ownership precision, false-MISSING count/rate on independently confirmed compliant instances, UNKNOWN rate, and per-class detection precision/recall. Proposed diagnostic gates: >=90% person coverage and >=95% correct ownership among assigned, assessable boxes; zero false MISSING on at least 20 confirmed compliant person-item examples. Review confidence intervals and do not infer general reliability from zero events in a small sample.
3. **Event and failure validation:** independently label at least five short clips spanning compliant, missing, occlusion, nearby workers and status transitions. Each must include timestamps and person/PPE/event IDs. Report event-level false incidents, missed events, duplicate episodes and identity switches, including errors/gaps. Proposed gate: all scripted error/UNKNOWN scenarios produce no unsupported incident, each continuous labelled event produces one candidate in stable unambiguous visibility, and every discrepancy is documented and addressed before alerts are considered.
4. **Detection and reproducibility:** on the adjudicated validation slice report per-class mAP/precision/recall and false positives/negatives at the actual operating thresholds; preserve a separate, source-aware held-out test set. Archive dataset version, split manifest, weights hash, environment, commands and hardware. Choose any further numeric detection gate before tuning; compare conditions and latency, not just aggregate metrics.
5. **Targeted fixes only:** add regression tests for demonstrated defects; fix association/rule/video behavior locally when evidence identifies it. Reassess training only after a corrected/representative data version demonstrates a detector-specific issue and a targeted experiment has a defined success criterion.

**Exact next action:** independently adjudicate the five already identified Step 4 cases and explicitly label PPE-box ownership, starting with the first-frame sample candidate and 00269 class-policy concern. Record results in a new review artifact; preserve the existing validation/test labels and all prior outputs.
